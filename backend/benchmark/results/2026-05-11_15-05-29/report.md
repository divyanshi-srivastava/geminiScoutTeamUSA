### Suggested Improvements
**Interview Quality**: Enforce a minimum turn count (e.g., 3-4 turns) and branching logic requirements before the pipeline is allowed to generate a final scouting report.

**Authenticity**: Integrate a sports-logic validation step to verify performance metrics (e.g., sprint times) and adaptive sports rules (e.g., Para Powerlifting lifts) against real-world data.

**Personalization**: Implement a strict context-check prompt to ensure the final narrative does not contradict the user's explicit interview choices, preventing issues seen in 05_youth_time_travel_2032.

**Life Stage Coherence**: Refine time-travel system prompts to strictly map the calculated age to specific developmental milestones (Rising Star, Elite Peak, Veteran) to prevent anachronistic phrasing.
